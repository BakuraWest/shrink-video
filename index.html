Download
Good to know
  • The first run downloads the encoder (about 30 MB). After that your browser caches it.
  • Encoding runs on your CPU inside the browser, so it's slower than a desktop app. A few minutes for a short clip is normal; a long or high-resolution video can take much longer.
  • The whole video is loaded into memory. Files over roughly 1 GB may fail, especially on phones or in older browsers. Desktop Chrome, Edge and Firefox work best.
  • Keep this tab open and in the foreground while it works.
  • Sizes are in MB as most upload sites count them (1 MB = 1,048,576 bytes). The result is aimed about 5% under your target.
